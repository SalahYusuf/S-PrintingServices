Ordering Flow

How to order.

This follows the ordering sequence from your existing SPS material, adapted into the website itself.

1 Open the form Use the quote section on this website instead of jumping between multiple pages.
2 Choose print options Select print type, sides, number of sheets, copies, lamination and fulfilment.
3 Fill in your details Add your name, MMU email or preferred contact details and any order notes.
4 Confirm the order Review the calculated estimate and save the order request in the demo portal.
5 Done Use self-pickup or door-to-door delivery based on the selected fulfilment method.
